Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

About Gunnar Assistant

Gunnar Assistant is a private, non-commercial personal assistant application used by its owner to access and manage personal information and Google services.

Google user data

Gunnar Assistant may access Google account data that the user explicitly authorizes through Google OAuth. Depending on the permissions granted, this may include Gmail, Google Calendar, and Google Tasks data.

How data is used

Google user data is used only to provide the personal assistant functionality requested by the authorized user, such as reading and managing email, calendar events, and tasks.

Data sharing

Google user data is not sold, rented, or used for advertising. The application is intended for private personal use and does not provide Google user data to unrelated third parties.

Data storage and security

Authentication credentials and application data are stored in systems controlled by the owner of Gunnar Assistant. Reasonable measures are used to protect credentials and personal information from unauthorized access.

Revoking access

Access to Google account data can be revoked at any time through the security settings of the relevant Google Account.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be sent to: gunnar.assistent@gmail.com
